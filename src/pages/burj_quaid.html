Burj Quaid

Where Pakistan Touches the Sky

A tribute to the enduring legacy of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah by Iqbal Estate.

Soaring 941 feet above the Karachi skyline, Burj Quaid is destined to be Pakistan’s tallest skyscraper and a definitive architectural marvel. Conceived as a premier lifestyle destination, it seamlessly integrates ultra-luxury residences, state-of-the-art corporate suites, high-end retail boutiques, and unparalleled leisure experiences—all anchored within the elite enclave of DHA City Karachi.

941 FT Height • 82 Storeys • CAA, PAA & DHA Approved

Exclusively Marketed by Discover Pakistan
