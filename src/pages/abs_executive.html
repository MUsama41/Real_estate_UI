ABS Executive

A tribute to vision, ambition, and architectural excellence.

Rising prominently in the heart of Lahore, ABS Executive is set to become Lahore’s premier executive address and one of the city’s most sought-after residential landmarks. Designed as a world-class luxury residential development by Iqbal Estate, it brings together elegantly crafted apartments, exceptional lifestyle amenities, and unmatched connectivity — all strategically positioned at the centre of Lahore near the prestigious DHA community and the Lahore Ring Road Interchange.

Prime Location at the Centre of Lahore • Unmatched Connectivity • Smart Investment in a High-Growth Corridor

TRAVEL DISTANCE TO ABS EXECUTIVE (LAHORE)

  • 2 minutes from DHA Phase 4, 5 & 6

  • 15 minutes from M2 Motorway
  • 15 minutes from Gulberg & Ferozepur Road
